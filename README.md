## Project Vision

VoteHub is a collaborative decision-making platform designed for communities, teams, and organizations to make collective choices efficiently. Unlike traditional polling systems, VoteHub focuses on facilitating group consensus through structured discussions and multi-stage voting processes.

## Features

### Advanced Voting Systems
- **Ranked Choice Voting** - Prevent vote splitting with instant-runoff voting
- **Quadratic Voting** - Allocate voting credits for more nuanced preferences
- **Consensus Tracking** - Visualize agreement levels and identify sticking points


### Analytics & Insights
- **Sentiment Analysis** - AI-powered analysis of discussion sentiment
- **Participation Metrics** - Track engagement and identify inactive members
- **Decision History** - Archive of past decisions with outcomes and lessons learned
